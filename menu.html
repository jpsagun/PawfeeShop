Rental Terms

  • Reserve ahead. Book through the Book Now page. Walk-ins depend on availability.
  • Billed per hour. 30-minute minimum, then charged in 30-minute blocks.
  • ID and waiver. A valid ID and a signed waiver are required before every rental.
  • Be gentle. No pulling tails or ears, and please don’t wake sleeping pets.
  • No outside food. Only staff-approved treats. Please don’t feed pets human food.
  • Keep it clean. Sanitize your hands before and after playing with our pets.
  • Pets come first. A rental may end early if a pet shows signs of stress or tiredness.
  • Be responsible. Guests 12 and under need an adult. Free cancellation up to 2 hours before.
